2. PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Develop an Explainable Retrieval-Augmented Generation system that verifies pharmaceutical regulatory compliance by comparing organizational documents against applicable regulatory requirements.

The system should accept:

Pharmaceutical regulatory documents.
Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
Quality Management System documents.
Good Manufacturing Practice documentation.
Equipment qualification records.
Process validation records.
Training records.
Audit reports.
Pharmaceutical quality documents.

The system must retrieve relevant regulatory requirements and compare them against organizational documents.

For every regulatory requirement, determine whether the organizational evidence is:

Compliant.
Partially compliant.
Non-compliant.
Not applicable.
Insufficient evidence.

Every generated conclusion must include traceable evidence and an explanation.

The system must never fabricate regulations, sections, page numbers, evidence, or citations.

This application is a regulatory decision-support system. Final compliance decisions must remain subject to qualified human review.

13. CORE COMPLIANCE VERIFICATION ENGINE

This is the central research contribution.

Build a requirement-by-requirement comparison engine.

Inputs:

For every requirement:

Compare the requirement and evidence.
Identify missing information.
Detect contradictions.
Identify partial coverage.
Generate a structured assessment.
Attach source references.
Calculate evidence coverage.
Generate an explanation.

Compliance statuses:

COMPLIANT PARTIALLY_COMPLIANT NON_COMPLIANT NOT_APPLICABLE INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE

Classification rules:

COMPLIANT: Sufficient, relevant, traceable evidence supports that the requirement is addressed.

PARTIALLY_COMPLIANT: Evidence addresses some but not all material elements of the requirement.

NON_COMPLIANT: Reliable evidence demonstrates a contradiction, failure, or omission against an applicable requirement within the assessed scope.

NOT_APPLICABLE: The requirement is demonstrably outside the documented assessment scope or its applicability conditions are not met, with an explanation.

INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE: The available documentation cannot establish whether the requirement is satisfied.

Do not classify missing documentation automatically as a proven operational violation.

Separate:

Missing documented evidence.
Confirmed process deficiency.
Contradictory evidence.
Unverified applicability.
Out-of-scope findings.

Every finding must contain:

{ "finding_id": "", "requirement_id": "", "requirement_text": "", "compliance_status": "", "regulatory_evidence": [], "organizational_evidence": [], "evidence_gaps": [], "contradictions": [], "reasoning_summary": "", "risk_level": "", "recommended_action": "", "review_status": "PENDING" }

Store every finding in PostgreSQL.
